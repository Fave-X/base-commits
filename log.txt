My first commit for Base. So as means of my little contribution to the BASE NETWORK, I'll be  dropping several ways on how to interaact with the base ecosystem
Download the official Base App (rebranded Coinbase Wallet super app).
Create or import a self-custody wallet inside the Base App.
Add your X and GitHub profiles in the app for better on-chain reputation.
Mint a Basename (your .base name) for identity on the network.
Bridge ETH or USDC from Ethereum mainnet via the official bridge.base.org.
Use alternative bridges like Rhino.fi, Stargate, or Wormhole for multi-chain entry.
Withdraw assets directly from Coinbase exchange to Base network if supported.
Switch your wallet network to Base Mainnet (Chain ID: 8453).
Add Base manually in MetaMask: RPC https://mainnet.base.org, explorer basescan.org.
Fund your wallet with small amounts of ETH for gas (Base fees are very low)
Explore the Explore tab in the Base App for trending dApps and mini-apps.
Swap tokens on integrated DEXs like Uniswap or Aerodrome directly in the app.
Trade on GeckoTerminal via the Base App Trade tab for mobile-first charting
Provide liquidity on Aerodrome — the leading Base DEX for yield farming.
Lend and borrow assets on Aave to generate on-chain activity.
Stake or farm on other DeFi protocols active on Base for points and rewards.
Mint free or low-cost NFTs on platforms like Mint.fun or ecosystem partners.
